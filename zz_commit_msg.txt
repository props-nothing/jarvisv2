fix(diagnostics): resolve the unix-only verbose_bit_mask lint

The annotations finally showed the actual lint, which was never a compile error on the
last run:

  crates/jarvis-diagnostics/src/doctor.rs:397:8: error: bit mask could be simplified
  with a call to `trailing_zeros`: help: try: `mode.trailing_zeros() >= 6`

`Permissions::mode()` returns `u32` while the `0o077` literal infers a signed type, so
`mode & 0o077 == 0` trips `clippy::verbose_bit_mask`. The lint exists only in the unix
build, so windows-latest linted clean while ubuntu-latest and macos-latest failed.

Both sites are rewritten in the lint's own recommended form rather than with a different
bit trick, so a neighbouring lint cannot replace one problem with another:

- `doctor::world_accessible`: `mode.trailing_zeros() >= 6`
- `repair::insecure_directories`: `mode.trailing_zeros() < 6`

Six is the correct shift because the low six bits of a permission mode are the group and
other bits; the owner bits are the next three. The doc comments record that reasoning and
why the obvious bitwise form fails only on unix.

This is the fourth distinct unix-only defect in this sequence, and the first found by
reading a diagnostic instead of inferring one.
